About Growth Day

Growth is evidence of life.

Growth Day is a high-impact, full-day locally-hosted leadership experience powered by FIRE Church and Denis Ekobena's global leadership platform, EQUIP2LEAD. A catalytic one-day experience — and the doorway into a guided journey of intentional personal, spiritual, and leadership growth.

The question is not whether you have potential. The question is how much of it you are maximizing — daily.

Most people don't fail for lack of talent. They fail for lack of intentionality, capacity, and consistency. Growth Day exists to break that pattern. In one high-impact day, attendees are confronted, equipped, and activated — not just inspired. And from that day, they are invited into a 6-Week Growth Journey, daily coaching, live seminars, and a community of growers who refuse to stay the same.

Growth is not an event — it's a process. Growth Day is where the process begins.
Who is this for

Done with stagnation. Ready to advance.

Growth Day is for anyone who is done with average mode and ready to advance forward — spiritually, mentally, professionally, and relationally.

Believers

Who want to move from potential to performance and live out the fullness God placed in them.

Leaders & emerging leaders

Who want to multiply their influence rather than just expand their workload.

Professionals & entrepreneurs

Seeking clarity, confidence, and consistency in their craft.

Pastors & ministry leaders

Who need to grow themselves so they can grow others.

Students & young adults

Preparing for the future they sense is ahead of them.

Anyone tired of average mode

Anyone who senses there is more, but doesn't yet have the system to access it.

"If you influence people — at work, at home, in your community — you are a leader. This is for you."

Vision & mission

Why we exist.

Our vision

To raise a generation of people who are bigger on the inside than on the outside.

Men and women of deep character, expanded capacity, and intentional growth, who influence their world with excellence and multiply that growth in others.

A generation that doesn't drift. A generation that grows on purpose.
Our mission

To help people discover, develop, and deploy their God-given potential.

  • 1.
    Catalytic experiences that ignite growth (Growth Day events, three times a year).
  • 2.
    Structured journeys that sustain it (the 6-Week Growth Cohort).
  • 3.
    Daily rhythms that compound it (Daily Growth audio, the 1% Practice).
  • 4.
    Transformational community that protects it (live bi-weekly seminars, accountability triads).
  • 5.
    Practical tools and timeless principles that anchor growth in truth, not trends.
We exist so that no one in our reach grows by accident.
Our core convictions

The eight non-negotiables that shape everything we do.

01

Growth is intentional, never accidental.

Calendars guarantee you will get older. Only growth guarantees you will get better.

02

Capacity precedes blessing.

What you can carry determines what you can be entrusted with.

03

1% a day, every day.

Small, consistent steps compound into transformed lives.

04

Growth must be measured.

What is not tracked is not grown.

05

Growth is C.L.E.A.R.

Communication, Leadership, Equipping, Attitude, Relationships — the five universal dimensions of every life worth growing.

06

Inner growth before outer impact.

True growth is not what people applaud, but what is built in silence.

Our posture

Convictions we hold openly.

Purpose-Driven Faith-Informed Inclusive

The Growth Day ecosystem

Three altitudes. One journey.

Growth Day is not just one day. It's an ecosystem of growth designed at three altitudes — Event, Journey, and Daily Rhythm — so that growth is no longer something you attend, but something you live.

🔥
Altitude 1
The Event

Growth Day

Frequency: 3 times per year

A one-day catalytic experience. Powerful teaching, interactive activations, real connection, and a clear call to commit to the next 6 weeks.

This is the doorway.
🌳
Altitude 2
The Journey

The 6-Week Growth Cohort

Launches after each Growth Day · ~30 participants per cohort · paid program

A guided 6-week program for committed participants. Each person builds their personal growth plan, chooses 2 areas to grow in, and walks through it with a coach, a guide, and a community.

What's included
  • A daily 5-min audio — "The Daily Growth"
  • Growth Journal & 1% Card
  • Bi-weekly live seminars (3 over 6 weeks)
  • Accountability triad — 2 peers + a Growth Guide
  • A graduation moment — testimonies & declarations
  • Lifelong access to the alumni community
This is where the transformation happens.

🌟 The Spiritual Growth Track

Optional add-on inside the Cohort

For participants who choose spiritual growth as one of their two areas, we offer a deeper bundle:

  • A guided Bible reading + meditation plan for the 6 weeks
  • An additional weekly Spiritual Growth devotional in audio form
  • Specific exercises on prayer, surrender, character, and capacity
  • A prayer partner within the cohort
  • A closing consecration moment at graduation

You don't have to choose this track. But if you do, the full package is yours.

🌱
Altitude 3
The Rhythm

The Daily 1% Practice

Lifelong, for alumni and the community

Even after the 6 weeks end, growers stay connected through:

  • The Daily Growth audio (continued for alumni)
  • Monthly check-in seminars
  • The community of growers and Growth Guides
  • Eligibility for the "Own Your Growth" Mastermind
This is the lifestyle.

Together, these three altitudes turn Growth Day from an event you remember into a rhythm that reshapes you.
